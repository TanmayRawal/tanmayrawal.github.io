Background

Software depth.
Hardware fluency.

I'm a researcher and builder working at the intersection of AI, embedded systems, and neuroimaging. What separates me from most ML students is that I don't just train models — I deploy them on hardware. Real chips, real speedups, real systems.

My primary research focus is Edge AI for medical neuroimaging — building CNN-based diagnostic systems that run on FPGA devices via Vitis AI, achieving hospital-grade accuracy at millisecond inference speeds. This work has been published at IHCIC 2025 (First Author) and is currently under review at ISVLSI 2026.

In parallel, I build full-stack Agentic AI systems — multi-agent RAG pipelines, MCP servers, and LLM-powered applications — combining my hardware depth with production-grade software engineering.

I work under the supervision of Dr. Kusum Lata, HOD of ECE at LNMIIT and a PhD alumna of IISc Bangalore, one of India's premier research institutions.

Research output

Peer-reviewed work at international venues on edge-deployed AI for medical neuroimaging.

IHCIC 2025
Published · First Author
INSIGHT-BRAIN: An Intelligent Ensemble-Based Multi-Class Brain Tumor Classification and Explainable AI System for Edge Deployment
International Conference on Intelligent Healthcare Computing and Innovation (IHCIC) · 2025 · Tanmay Rawal et al.

Developed a multi-class brain tumor MRI classification system using an ensemble of EfficientNetB0, InceptionV3, and Xception with quantitative XAI (Grad-CAM, LIME, SHAP). Deployed on PYNQ-ZU FPGA via Vitis AI with INT8 quantization achieving 98.8% accuracy at production-grade inference throughput. First end-to-end neuroimaging AI-to-hardware pipeline for clinical edge deployment.

First Author 98.8% Accuracy EfficientNetB0 · InceptionV3 · Xception Vitis AI · PYNQ-ZU Grad-CAM · LIME · SHAP INT8 Quantization
ISVLSI 2026
Under Review
FPGA-Accelerated Alzheimer's Disease Detection via Quantized ResNet50 on PYNQ-ZU with Real-Time Edge Inference
IEEE International Symposium on VLSI (ISVLSI) · 2026 · Tanmay Rawal et al.

Full-pipeline Alzheimer's MRI classification using ResNet50 with Focal Loss training and two-phase fine-tuning on merged OASIS+Falah dataset. Deployed on PYNQ-ZU via Vitis AI achieving 96.42% accuracy, 98.4 FPS inference throughput, and 13.9× CPU speedup. Includes live webcam detection on FPGA hardware.
